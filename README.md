  Retail Sales Analysis Dashboard | Power BI

##  Project Overview
The **Retail Sales Analysis Dashboard** is an interactive data visualization project developed using Microsoft Power BI to analyze retail sales performance across products, brands, stores, and time periods. The dashboard provides a consolidated view of key business metrics, helping users understand sales trends, compare product performance, and explore retail data through interactive visualizations.

##  Project Objectives
- Monitor overall sales performance using key performance indicators (KPIs).
- Analyze monthly sales trends to identify fluctuations and patterns.
- Compare sales performance across product categories and brands.
- Identify top-performing products based on total sales.
- Explore sales distribution across stores and geographic locations.
- Enable dynamic analysis using interactive slicers and filters.

##  Tools & Technologies
- **Microsoft Power BI Desktop** – Dashboard development and visualization.
- **Power Query** – Data cleaning and transformation.
- **DAX (Data Analysis Expressions)** – KPI calculations and business metrics.
- **Data Modeling** – Organizing sales, product, customer, store, and calendar data.
- **Data Visualization** – Line charts, bar charts, treemaps, maps, and KPI cards.

##  Key Dashboard Features
- **KPI Cards:** Displays total sales (63M), total orders (381), total customers (5K), total stores (5K), and a percentage-based metric.
- **Monthly Sales Trends:** Visualizes changes in sales across different months.
- **Category Analysis:** Highlights sales performance by product category.
- **Brand Comparison:** Compares brand-level sales, including Epson and Canon.
- **Top Products Analysis:** Identifies high-performing products based on total sales.
- **Geographic Visualization:** Displays location-based information for exploring regional sales distribution.
- **Store-Level Analysis:** Compares sales, profit, and total cost across selected stores.
- **Interactive Filters:** Allows users to filter the dashboard by city, category, and brand.

##  Skills Demonstrated

 Project Outcome
Developed a visually engaging, interactive retail dashboard that transforms sales data into meaningful business insights and supports analysis of product performance, brand comparisons, monthly trends, and store-level metrics.
